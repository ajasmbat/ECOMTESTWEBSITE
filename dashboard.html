What do these words mean?

Click to show the cheat sheet
Visitor
One person on their browser. If they open the shop on their phone AND laptop, that's 2 visitors.
Source
Where the visitor came from — a Facebook ad, a Google search ad, an email, or they typed the URL themselves.
Pageview
Somebody just landed on a page of the shop.
Click
Somebody clicked a button or link on a page.
Behind-the-scenes
The website whispered something to Google Tag Manager (like "the page is loaded" or "they added something to the cart"). You didn't see it happen — the code did it.
click_id
A fingerprint the ad platform (Facebook, Google) attaches to a click so they can later say "yes — that click I sent you turned into a sale."
UTM (source / medium / campaign)
Little name-tags glued onto the URL by the marketer so the shop knows which ad brought this person in.

Welcome! This is your live shop watcher.

Whenever somebody lands on your shop, clicks something, or does anything at all, it will pop up here in real time — with a plain-English description of what happened and where they came from.

Try it right now:

  1. Open this dashboard in one tab and leave it here.
  2. In a new tab, go to http://localhost:8002/ (the fake Facebook ad).
  3. Click the ad's button. The shop opens with a name-tag that says "this person came from Facebook."
  4. Click around the shop — add stuff to the cart, go to checkout. Every single click shows up on this dashboard.
  5. Come back here to see it all!
